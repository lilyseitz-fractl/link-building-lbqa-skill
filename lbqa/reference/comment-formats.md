# Comment formats — paste-ready notes for the DJ / designer

These are the notes the human will copy out of the chat and paste into a Google Doc
comment (on the WU) or a comment on an image (for the designer). The human is doing
the verification; your job is to hand them a note they can paste **without editing**.

## First decide: does the DJ/designer need to weigh in?

- **Clear-cut fix the CM doc or PC already settles** (title-case correction, documented terminology swap, a value that must match the PC): **skip the templates — hand back ONLY the corrected full text/value.** The human comments that corrected string directly onto the image or doc, and that's how the designer/DJ knows what to change. No "the subtitle reads X; per title case it should be Y; please update" — just `Y`.
- **Needs a judgment call** (an unverifiable or ambiguous claim, a number reconcilable more than one way, a qualifier with several valid fixes, anything the docs don't settle): use the explanatory templates below so the DJ has the context to decide.

## What makes a good comment

- **Specific.** Name the exact element and the exact numbers. "the 47% in the top-left
  stat callout," not "one of the stats." "Section 3 / second paragraph," not "the body."
- **Self-contained.** The DJ/designer sees only the comment, not this chat. Include both
  the current (wrong) value and the correct one so they can act without asking back.
- **One issue per comment.** If an image has three problems, write three comments. The
  designer resolves them one at a time and checks each off.
- **Action-first, no throat-clearing.** State the problem and the fix. No "It appears
  that…," "I noticed that…," "Just flagging…," or other AI padding.
- **Quote, don't paraphrase.** When you reference WU or PC wording, quote it verbatim so
  there's no ambiguity about what you're pointing at.

Keep each comment to one or two sentences. These are margin notes, not paragraphs.

## WU comments (left in the Google Doc, addressed to the DJ)

Use these when the human asks where a WU claim lives in the PC and you've searched.

**A — Claim is not in the PC at all**
> Not in the PC. This figure/claim doesn't appear anywhere in the PC — please add the
> supporting data to the PC, or confirm and link the source so it can be verified.

Example (filled in):
> Not in the PC. The "62% of renters" stat in the intro isn't in the PC — please add the
> supporting data or confirm the source.

**B — Claim is in the PC but the number/wording doesn't match (a real discrepancy)**
> WU says "[exact WU wording]"; the PC shows "[exact PC wording]." Please confirm which is
> correct and align the two.

Example:
> WU says "nearly half (41%)"; the PC shows "41%." 41% isn't "nearly half" (that band is
> ~45–49%) — please reword to "over 2 in 5 (41%)" or correct the figure.

**C — Claim is in the PC but a qualifier was added/changed that the data doesn't support**
> The PC supports the figure but not the qualifier. PC shows "[PC text]"; the WU calls this
> "[WU qualifier]." Please align the wording to what the data supports.

**D — Stat appears in the WU but no source / the PC's source is paywalled or weak**
> Source check: this claim needs a verifiable source. The PC doesn't include one — please
> add a primary-source link (or confirm it's from the study itself).

## Image comments (left on the infographic, addressed to the DJ/designer)

Use these after comparing the image's text against the PC and the CM doc.

**E — Number on the asset doesn't match the PC**
> The [location, e.g. "top-left stat callout"] reads "[image value]" but the PC shows
> "[PC value]." Please update to [PC value].

Example:
> The headline stat reads "47%" but the PC shows "45%." Please update to 45%.

**F — Label / category / ranking on the asset is wrong**
> The [chart/label location] lists [what the image says], but the PC ranks these as
> [correct order/labels per the PC]. Please correct.

Example:
> The bar chart orders the cities Austin > Denver > Phoenix, but the PC ranks them
> Denver > Austin > Phoenix. Please reorder to match the PC.

**G — Stat on the asset isn't in the PC**
> The "[image stat]" on this asset doesn't appear in the PC. Please confirm the figure
> against the data and add it to the PC, or remove it from the asset.

**H — CM-doc / branding issue on the asset**
> Per the CM doc, [the rule — e.g. "the client name is styled 'LoanDepot,' one word"].
> This asset shows "[what's on the asset]." Please update.

Example:
> Per the CM doc, percentages on assets use a whole number with no decimal. This asset
> shows "44.7%." Please round to 45%.

**I — Missing required element on the asset (source line, methodology, fair-use, logo)**
> The CM doc requires [element] on every asset. This one is missing it — please add
> "[exact required text]."

**J — Spelling / grammar error (WU doc comment or image comment)**
> Typo: "[exact text as written]" → should be "[correction]."

Examples:
> Typo: "the the most vulnerable" → should be "the most vulnerable."
> Grammar: "the metros that faces the most heat" → should be "the metros that face the most heat."

---

## When the asset is clean

Don't invent issues to look thorough. If the image matches the PC and the CM doc, say so
plainly in the chat (no comment to leave):
> ✅ This asset matches the PC and follows the CM doc — no edits needed.
